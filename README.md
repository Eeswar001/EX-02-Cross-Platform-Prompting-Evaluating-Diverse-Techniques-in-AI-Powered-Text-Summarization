# EX-02-Cross-Platform-Prompting-Evaluating-Diverse-Techniques-in-AI-Powered-Text-Summarization

## AIM
To evaluate and compare the effectiveness of prompting techniques (zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-based) across different AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) in a specific task: text summarization.

## Scenario:
You are part of a content curation team for an educational platform that delivers quick summaries of research papers to undergraduate students. Your task is to summarize a 500-word technical article on "The Basics of Blockchain Technology" using multiple AI platforms and prompting strategies.

Your goal is to determine which combination of prompting technique + platform provides the best summary in terms of:

Accuracy : How well the generated summary preserves key information and meaning.

Coherence : The logical flow and readability of the summarized content.

Simplicity : How easily the summarized content can be understood by undergraduate readers.

Speed : The time taken by the platform to generate the summary.

User experience : Ease of use, interface quality, and customization of responses.

## Algorithm
1) Selection of Platforms:
   Choose four AI platforms — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot — for comparison.
   
2) Selection of Prompting Techniques:
   Use the following prompting methods across all platforms:
      a) Zero-shot prompting: Directly ask the AI to summarize the article without examples.

      b) Few-shot prompting: Provide a few examples of good summaries before requesting the new one.

      c) Chain-of-thought prompting: Ask the AI to explain its reasoning process step-by-step before producing the final summary.

      d) Role-based prompting: Instruct the AI to act as a specific role (e.g., “You are an educational content writer summarizing for college students”).    

3) Input Preparation:
   Take a 500-word technical passage on “The Basics of Blockchain Technology” and use it as the standard input text for all experiments.
   
4) Execution of Prompts:
   Run each prompting technique on every AI platform individually and collect the summaries produced.

5) Evaluation Metrics:
   Assess each summary using the following metrics:

      a) Accuracy: Check if key blockchain concepts like decentralization, distributed ledger, blocks, consensus, and cryptography are correctly represented.

      b) Coherence: Evaluate sentence flow and structure.

      c) Simplicity: Measure readability and clarity for undergraduate students.

      d) Speed: Record time taken to generate the response.

      e) User Experience: Note platform interface ease, customization, and formatting features.
